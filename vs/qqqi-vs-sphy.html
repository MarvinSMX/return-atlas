QQQI vs SPHY: The Verdict

On average annual total return since inception, QQQI decisively outperforms SPHY: +18.8% per year vs +4.9% per year — a gap of 13.9 percentage points annually. Total return counts both price movement and dividends, so this is the whole picture of each fund's performance, not just yield.
