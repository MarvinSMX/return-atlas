❓ DVY FAQ

What is the dividend yield of DVY?
DVY currently shows a distribution yield of 3.3%. Yield reflects the most recent distribution annualized and can change with every payment.
What is the total return of DVY since inception?
DVY has returned 604% in total since it launched on 11/3/2003, which works out to roughly 8.90% per year on average.
Is the DVY yield sustainable?
Total return is comfortably ahead of the distribution rate — this fund has been growing capital while paying you. A distribution rate that runs persistently ahead of total return generally means capital is being returned rather than earned.
How is the DVY grade calculated?
Our A- grade combines three things: annualized total return (50 points), cumulative total return since inception (25 points), and whether annualized return keeps pace with the distribution rate (25 points). DVY scored 79 out of 100. This is our opinion only, not investment advice.
